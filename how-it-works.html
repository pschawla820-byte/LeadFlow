Simple & Transparent

From enquiry to booked appointment
— completely automatic

Here's exactly what happens the moment someone enquires about your business. No jargon. No complicated tech. Just results.

01

A lead comes in from anywhere

Someone sees your property listing, your clinic's ad, or your coaching institute's social post. They message you — on WhatsApp, fill out your website form, or DM you on Instagram or Facebook.

LeadFlow AI is connected to all these channels. The moment a message arrives, it's picked up instantly — even if it's 2 AM on a Sunday.

02

Your AI responds instantly

In under 10 seconds, the lead receives a warm, personalised reply. It doesn't sound robotic — it sounds like a helpful team member who knows your business inside out.

The AI introduces your business, answers basic questions, and starts a natural conversation to understand what the lead is looking for — all while you're doing literally anything else.

03

The lead gets qualified automatically

Through a friendly back-and-forth conversation, the AI gathers everything you need to know: their budget, specific requirements, timeline, and how serious they are.

Each lead is automatically scored and tagged — Hot, Warm, or Cold — so you and your team know exactly who deserves your immediate attention and who needs a slower nurture.

  • Budget & requirements collected automatically
  • No awkward cold calls needed
  • Only serious leads reach your sales team
  • Saves 3–5 hours of manual follow-up per day

04

Appointment booked. Job done.

Once qualified, the AI guides the lead to choose an appointment slot from your real-time calendar availability. They book it themselves — no phone calls, no WhatsApp scheduling conversations.

They receive a confirmation on WhatsApp and a reminder the day before. You receive a notification with full lead details. You just show up and close the deal.

  • Integrates with Google Calendar & Calendly
  • Automatic confirmation & reminder messages
  • Reduces no-shows by up to 40%
  • You focus on the meeting, not the logistics
