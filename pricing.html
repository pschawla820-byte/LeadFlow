Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any technical knowledge to use LeadFlow AI?

Not at all. Our team handles the entire setup — from connecting your WhatsApp to training the AI. You don't need to know anything about technology.

How long does it take to get started?

Most clients are fully live within 5–7 business days. This includes onboarding, AI training, integrations, and testing.

What happens if I exceed my monthly lead limit?

We'll notify you and discuss upgrading your plan. We won't cut off your service mid-month — your leads will still be captured.

Can the AI speak in Hindi or other languages?

Yes. Growth and Pro plans support multi-language conversations. We can set up Hindi, Hinglish, or any regional language on request.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

No long-term contracts. All plans are monthly and you can cancel anytime. We also offer a 30-day money-back guarantee.

What CRMs and tools do you integrate with?

We integrate with Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Google Calendar, Calendly, WhatsApp Business API, Instagram, and Facebook. Custom integrations available on Pro.
