Automatic Appointment Booking

Once a lead is qualified, they're guided seamlessly to book a slot that suits them — directly in your calendar. No phone tag, no back-and-forth messages, no forgotten follow-ups.

Whether it's a property site visit, a dental consultation, or a demo class — the AI handles the entire scheduling process from start to confirmation.

  • Syncs with Google Calendar, Calendly & more
  • Sends booking confirmation via WhatsApp & SMS
  • Automatic reminders — reduces no-shows by 40%
  • Reschedule & cancellation handled by AI
  • Collects advance payment (optional) at booking
